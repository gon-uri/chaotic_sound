# How fast can a parameter be modulated?

Script: `lab/scripts/studies/modulation.py`. Run it from `lab/` with
`caffeinate -is uv run python scripts/studies/modulation.py`; it takes 239 s wall time on an M1.
Numbers are in `lab/outputs/studies/modulation/results.json`. Figures and WAVs are in the same folder
(regenerated by the script, gitignored): `fig1_phase_profiles.png`, `fig2_rate_summary.png`,
`fig3_jump_response.png`, `fig4_spectra.png`, `fig5_chua_escape.png` and `wav/*.wav`.

## Question

When u is modulated, dx/dτ = F(x; p(u(t))) is non-autonomous. Pitch lock (k = P(u)·f₀) and
normalization ((x − μ(u))/σ(u)) read tables measured at **fixed** u, so they assume the
trajectory follows the attractor adiabatically. How fast can u move before:

- (a) the heard pitch leaves the note;
- (b) the RMS leaves 0.25;
- (c) the trajectory leaves the attractor or triggers safety resets?

Where is the adiabatic regime, where does genuine forcing start, and what is dangerous?

**Rate unit.** One *sweep* is a full traversal 0 → 1 (half a sine cycle). The rate is
r = sweeps per oscillation period of the attractor = 2/(f₀T) for a full-range sine with LFO
period T. Its inverse, N = f₀T/2, is the number of oscillation periods per sweep. At A3
(220 Hz), T = 20 s gives r = 4.5·10⁻⁴ (N = 2200), and T = 0.05 s gives r = 0.18 (N = 5.5).

## Method

- **Systems.** Lorenz (ρ ∈ [24.8, 71.3]; P changes by a factor of 1.9), Chua (α ∈ [13.7, 18.05]:
  single scroll, then double scroll; escape at α ≈ 18.45) and Rössler (c ∈ [5.5, 8.8]). Each uses
  its default observable (z, y and x), note A3, 48 kHz, and the real engine path
  (`Dynamical.process`, including the 5 ms declick and the 5 Hz DC blocker). The script wraps
  `render_tuned` to record the state and the per-sample parameter without changing engine code.
  As a cross-check, the same Lorenz patch built through `Patch.render` (chunked, Output block)
  is bit-identical below the soft-clip knee and reports `safety_resets = {'osc': 0}`.
- **Drive.** A full-range sine modulator with T ∈ {20, 10, 5, 2, 1, 0.5, 0.2, 0.1, 0.05} s. Two
  extra periods, 0.02 and 0.01 s, go beyond the brief to probe the audio-rate side. Each run uses
  3 seeds, a 2 s transient, and then ≥ 30 s (at least 2 LFO cycles) analysed in 24 LFO-phase bins.
- **Static reference.** Fixed u on 41 grid points, 10 s each, measured with exactly the same
  estimators. Each modulated bin is compared with this static value at the same u(t). The
  declicked u(t) is used, so the 5 ms smoothing lag is not counted as non-adiabaticity.
- **(a) Pitch.** The audio is band-passed (f₀/1.6 … 1.6 f₀) and Hilbert transformed. The heard
  frequency in a bin is the instantaneous frequency averaged with weight |z|². This average
  equals the spectral centroid of the band in that bin, and it resolves about one oscillation
  period (≈ 5 ms). The up-sweep bin and the down-sweep bin at the same u are combined into a
  *hysteresis* part, (up − down)/2, which is odd in du/dt, and a *symmetric* part.
- **(b) Loudness.** The audio RMS per bin, in dB re 0.25. Also measured: the mean of
  s = (x_obs − μ(u))/σ(u) before the DC blocker (ideally 0), and the *modulation-locked LF
  component*, the RMS of the LFO-synchronous average of the audio low-passed below f₀/2.
- **(c) Attractor.** We count safety resets, and record the largest |x − centre|/half-width
  over the safety box ("box usage", which is 1 at a reset). We also measure the
  nearest-neighbour distance from the state to the static attractor clouds at the bracketing
  grid u. The clouds are symmetrized for Lorenz and Chua, and distances are in units of the
  static σ per variable. For scale, the 99.9 % quantile of an independent static run is
  0.05–0.065 σ.
- **Extras.**
  - Square-wave jumps 0 ↔ 1, holding each level for 64 periods: 40 jumps × 3 seeds at A3
    and at A1.
  - A reset probe at A0, A1, A2 and A3 (60 up-jumps × 3 seeds each), plus two Chua mitigations.
  - Chua with observable x.
  - Lorenz at A2, A3 and A4 at the same N, to test whether "per oscillation period" is the
    right unit.

## Results

### Static floor (fixed u)

| system | pitch − note across u (this estimator) | RMS across u | peak audio | NN 99.9 % (σ) | box usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lorenz | −33 … +31 c | 0.247 … 0.253 | 0.99 | 0.049 | 0.33 |
| Chua | −70 … +16 c | 0.233 … 0.255 | 0.60 | 0.065 | 0.33 |
| Rössler | −57 … +12 c | 0.245 … 0.254 | 0.73 | 0.058 | 0.33 |

The absolute static numbers come from the broad-band estimator, not from the catalog's ±10 %
peak centroid, which agrees with the note to within a few cents near the default. They show
that the "pitch" of a broad chaotic peak is estimator-dependent at the ±30 c level, which is a
separate question from this study. Everything below therefore subtracts the static value at
the same u.

**Pitch, more detail.**

- **Absolute RMS against the note.** Lorenz: 19 c static → 19 (T = 1 s), 23 (0.2), 30 (0.1),
  41 (0.05). Rössler: 12–13 c up to T = 0.2 s, then 14 (0.1) and 26 (0.05). Chua stays at its
  static ≈ 20 c down to T = 0.5 s, then 30 (0.2) and 41 (0.05).
- **Chua at T = 1–2 s.** Here Chua's Δpitch rises without a rise in the absolute error.
  Narrow periodic windows (α ≈ 14.1, 15.35, 15.95, …) give the static curve dips of −40 c.
  At T = 2 s the sweep crosses such a window in about 1–2 periods, too fast to lock onto the
  periodic orbit, so the dip is skipped. The windows' sharp spectral lines also disappear from
  the long-term spectra (`fig4`).
- **Character of the error.** Up to r ≈ 0.1 the error is mostly hysteresis and grows about
  linearly with the rate. For Lorenz the hysteresis RMS is 5.9, 11.8 and 22.1 c at r = 0.018,
  0.045 and 0.091, which is about 240 c per unit r. Rössler is about 100 c per unit r and Chua
  about 170. In Lorenz the pitch runs sharp while ρ rises and flat while it falls (`fig1`).
  The cycle-averaged offset stays small: |mean| ≤ 7 c up to T = 0.05 s. From r ≈ 0.2 upward
  the symmetric part dominates (Lorenz at T = 0.02 s: hysteresis 10 c, symmetric 64 c): the
  response is phase-shifted, not a small lag.

**The right unit is periods, not seconds.** Lorenz at the same N:

| N | A2 | A3 | A4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (T = 0.2 / 0.1 / 0.05 s) | 25.3 c | 24.2 c | 20.9 c (the declick cuts the depth to 0.08–0.92) |
| 55 (T = 1 / 0.5 / 0.25 s) | 8.7 c | 7.7 c | 6.6 c |

The note-dependence is within about 20 %, and part of it is the fixed 5 ms declick. A rule
stated in r therefore transfers across notes.

- The overall RMS stays within ±0.3 dB of 0.25 down to T = 0.05 s, with one exception:
  Rössler is +0.44 dB at 0.05 s and +0.76 dB at 0.02 s.
- The per-phase level swings, again mostly hysteresis: Lorenz is louder on the up-sweep and
  quieter on the down-sweep.
- **The LF-locked component.** The orbit's mean lags μ(u(t)), so the normalization leaves a
  residue at f_mod and its harmonics. For Lorenz z, μ moves from 20 to 65 across the range,
  and the residue appears as a visible line at 20 Hz (T = 0.05 s) and at 50 Hz (0.02 s) in
  `fig4`. At −23 dB it is audible on full-range speakers but not dangerous. Below 5 Hz the
  DC blocker removes most of it. Chua y is at −41 dB or below at every rate.
- **Distance to the static attractor.** It is a very sensitive measure. Lorenz, the most
  dissipative system, stays on its static attractor (q99 = 0.026 σ) up to T = 0.5 s. Rössler's
  thin band and Chua's windows register lags already at T = 1–2 s (q99 ≈ 0.07–0.3 σ), long
  before anything is audible. The largest distance seen was 5.9 σ (Rössler, T = 0.02 s), with box
  usage 0.34. **Sine modulation produced no safety reset at any rate.**

### Jumps (square wave 0 ↔ 1; loop rate = maxima of the observable; `fig3`)

| system | jump | A3: pitch in 1st period | A3: settles within (±10 c or 2 SE) | A1: 1st period | A1: settles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lorenz | 0 → 1 | +212 c | 2 periods | +522 c | 6 |
| Lorenz | 1 → 0 | −361 c | 12 (then +33 c until ≥ 64) | −478 c | 12 |
| Chua | 0 → 1 | −161 c | 3 | −92 c | 2 |
| Chua | 1 → 0 | +27 c | 6 | +104 c | 4 |
| Rössler | 0 → 1 | −96 c | 12 | −95 c | 32 (noisy) |
| Rössler | 1 → 0 | +64 c | 8 | +153 c | 2 |

- Pitch lock changes k instantly, but the orbit needs 2–12 periods (10–55 ms at A3) to reach
  its new period. Each jump is therefore heard as a short pitch chirp of up to ±5 semitones.
- The jump is harder at A1 because the fixed 5 ms declick lasts 1.1 periods at A3 but only
  0.27 periods at A1.
- Lorenz after 1 → 0 relaxes slowly: it stays +33 c off for 32–64 periods. ρ = 24.8 sits
  just above the subcritical Hopf point (24.74), so long spirals around C± slow the approach.
  We read this as critical slowing near the bottom of the range, but it is not proven.
- The first-period normalization error is modest (|mean s| ≤ 0.9, i.e. ≤ 0.23 in audio).
  Lorenz post-jump peaks reach 0.99 at A3 and 1.15 at A1, against a static peak of 0.99, so
  the soft clip catches them at volume 1.

### Danger: Chua escapes after abrupt jumps at low notes (`fig5`)

| case (square wave, 64 periods per level) | resets / up-jumps | when (periods after the jump) |
|---|---|---|
| Chua 0 ↔ 1, A0 (27.5 Hz) | **6 / 180** | 3.9–6.3 |
| Chua 0 ↔ 1, A1 (55 Hz) | **3 / 180** | 5.6–6.3 |
| Chua 0 ↔ 1, A2 and A3 | 0 / 180 each | |
| Chua 0 ↔ 0.9 (α ≤ 17.6), A0 and A1 | 0 / 180 each | |
| Chua 0 ↔ 1 with an extra one-pole of τ = 2 periods on u, A0 and A1 | 0 / 180 each | |
| Lorenz and Rössler 0 ↔ 1, A0–A3 | 0 / 180 each (box usage ≤ 0.37 and ≤ 0.64) | |

- **Mechanism.** A state on the single scroll at α = 13.7 is thrown into α = 18.05 within a
  fraction of a period. There it apparently lies outside the basin of the α = 18.05 attractor (or on a long escaping transient): it
  spirals out over about 5 loops and hits the box.
- **What you hear.** The audio reaches about 1.8 before the reset (soft-clipped at the
  Output), then the reset makes a discontinuity: a pop. A WAV of one event is
  `wav/chua_square_A1_escape.wav`.
- **Statistics.** The counts are small. 0/180 means a rate below 1.7 % at 95 % confidence,
  and 6/180 against 0/180 gives a two-sided Fisher p ≈ 0.03. The mitigations are therefore suggestive,
  not proven.

### Minor: Chua with observable x or z

- In the single-scroll range (u ≲ 0.17) the catalog's μ_x = +1.18 (μ_z = −1.18) belongs to
  one of the two mirror scrolls. Under modulation the scroll is chosen afresh at every entry:
  7 of 18 entries (T = 5 s) landed on the other scroll, where s sits at −3.47, which is −0.87
  in audio units before the DC blocker.
- The 5 Hz DC blocker removes the offset within about 30 ms, so at T = 5 s the peaks only rise
  from about 0.8 to 0.9. It becomes a thump only for fast entries.
- The default observable y is symmetric and unaffected.

## Conclusion

1. **Adiabatic regime: r ≲ 0.01 sweeps per period** (a full sweep lasting ≥ 100 oscillation
   periods; a full-range sine with T ≥ 200/f₀, i.e. T ≥ 0.9 s at A3, 3.6 s at A1 and 0.23 s
   at A5).
   - Pitch is within about 2× the static noise floor of the static value (Lorenz ≤ 6 c,
     Rössler ≤ 2.5 c). Chua's absolute error is unchanged; its periodic windows are only
     partly heard.
   - Loudness stays within ±0.7 dB, the LF residue is ≤ −41 dB, and there are no resets.
   - The current default `period_s = 4 s` meets this down to about A1.
2. **Transitional regime: 0.01 ≲ r ≲ 0.1** (A3: 1 s ≳ T ≳ 0.1 s).
   - The pitch errs in the direction of the sweep (hysteresis), roughly linearly in the rate:
     ≈ 2.4 c (Lorenz), 1.7 c (Chua) and 1 c (Rössler) per 0.01 sweeps/period. That gives
     4–25 c RMS and ±0.4–1.4 dB.
   - The effect is audible as a slight "lean" of the pitch with the sweep, not as a broken
     tuning.
3. **Genuine forcing: r ≳ 0.1** (A3: T ≲ 0.1 s, f_mod ≳ 10 Hz).
   - Pitch errs by 13–41 c RMS at T = 0.1–0.05 s (peaks of 33–90 c) and by up to 65 c RMS
     beyond (peaks of 110 c), with a 10–50 Hz FM/AM-like roughness and ±1.1–2.7 dB level
     swings.
   - The carrier is broadened and gains sidebands, a line appears at f_mod (Lorenz), and the
     Chua and Rössler window lines vanish.
   - It remains stable and bounded with smooth (sine) modulation even at 100 Hz, and it is
     musically usable (listen to `wav/*_T0.05s_*`, `*_T0.02s_*`). It is not "pitch-locked"
     any more.
4. **Danger: discontinuous control** (square and ramp shapes: a full-range jump that the 5 ms
   declick spreads over less than about half an oscillation period) on Chua at low notes.
   Escapes with safety resets occurred in 3.3 % (A0) and 1.7 % (A1) of full-range up-jumps. Jumps produce 10–55 ms pitch chirps of up to ±5 semitones in
   every system.

## Recommendations

- **Do not cap `period_s` in the UI.** Fast modulation is a legitimate, stable effect. Show
  instead an informational badge computed from r = 2·depth/(f₀·T). Depth is currently always
  1; the depth scaling is untested but is the linear-response expectation.
  - r > 0.02: "pitch lock and loudness approximate".
  - r > 0.1: "audio-rate forcing: pitch and level not locked".
  - Explain in the educational text that the limit is counted in oscillation periods, so
    low notes reach it sooner.
- **Keep `period_s = 4 s` as the default.** r = 0.0023 at A3, and r ≤ 0.01 for every note at or
  above A1.
- **Engine (needs an ADR; not changed here).** Make abrupt control changes gentle in *system
  time*: for example, a declick or slew time constant of τ = max(5 ms, 2/f₀) on u. That
  removed the Chua escapes in the probe (0/180) and would make the jump chirps
  note-independent. The alternative or complement is to lower Chua's p_max: α ≤ 17.6
  (u ≤ 0.9) gave 0/180. The reset safety net works as designed, but it produces a pop.
- **Catalog.** For systems with mirror attractors (Chua's single scroll), either symmetrize μ
  for x and z (μ = 0, σ from the symmetrized data) or document that only y is safe under
  modulation through 13.7–14.6.
- **Follow-ups not done here.**
  - Partial-depth sweeps (to check that the rate rule works with depth).
  - Duffing and Thomas.
  - Other notes for Chua and Rössler.
  - Whether pitch perception of the 10–50 Hz roughness matches the centroid estimator.

## Uncertainties and limitations

- **Pitch noise floor.** Chaotic phase diffusion within a bin, together with the 41-point
  static reference, gives a floor of about 5 c (Lorenz), 6 c (Chua) and 1 c (Rössler) RMS.
  It is visible as the flat left part of `fig2`. Differences below about 2× the floor are not
  significant.
- **Smoothing at the fastest rates.** The estimator resolves about one period, so at
  T ≤ 0.05 s the phase profiles are slightly smoothed. The maxima are underestimates rather
  than overestimates.
- **Static reference grid.** The grid spacing Δu = 0.025 does not resolve narrow periodic
  windows. This inflates Chua's floor and the Chua and Rössler distances at slow rates.
- **Coverage.** Only full-range sine and square drives were tested, only A3 for the main
  sweep (A1–A4 for the checks), and 3 seeds. Seed spreads are small compared with the trends
  (see `results.json`).
- **Escape statistics.** The escape rates rest on small counts (above).
